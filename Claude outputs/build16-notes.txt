Build 16 — live call translation: live partial captions, and fixes for three
of the four reported problems.

Live captions while you speak (new)
- Subtitles and the chat message no longer wait for you to finish a
  sentence. As you talk, the words recognised so far appear on the other
  person's video and in a chat message ending in "…". When the sentence is
  final the message is completed, and a moment later both the subtitle line
  and the message switch to the translation.
- Only the finished sentence is translated; the in-progress text is never
  sent to the translator, so this costs nothing extra there.
- The chat message is updated at most every 2.5 seconds while you talk, so
  the chat is not flooded with edits; the subtitles update continuously.
- Each sentence has its own subtitle line, so a translation that takes
  longer lands on its line without shuffling the others.

Transcript not appearing until you made a noise
- I could not reproduce this against the service with recorded speech: a
  10-second sentence was finalised about a second after it ended every time.
  As insurance the app now keeps sending 1.5 seconds of "silence" after you
  stop talking (was 0.7), which is what the service listens for to decide a
  sentence is over. With live captions you will also now see whether the
  service heard you at all, which will tell us more if it happens again.


Translation stops working after a while (button had to be tapped twice)
- The link to the transcription service was never reopened once it dropped.
  It drops whenever nothing has been sent for about 45 seconds, and the app
  sends nothing while the *other* person is talking — so whoever listens
  longer (Ericka) lost it first. Everything said after that went into a dead
  link and never came back. Tapping off and on happened to open a new one.
- Now: after 45 seconds of silence the link is closed on purpose and the next
  word you say opens a fresh one (about a second). A link that goes dead
  mid-sentence is also noticed after 20 seconds of unanswered speech and
  replaced. Verified against the live service with recorded Spanish clips:
  close after idle, reconnect, and the last words before switching off are
  all delivered.

Translate button lit at call start but nothing happens
- The button remembered your last choice for that contact and showed ON,
  but nothing actually started the translation for it. It only ever started
  when you tapped the button during the call.
- Now: if it is saved as ON, translation starts by itself the moment the
  call connects (not during ringing). If the contact has no language set any
  more, the button shows OFF instead of lying.

Ericka heard as two speakers
- The service's speaker separation was splitting one voice in two, and
  everything it called "speaker 2" was treated as a bystander: put in the
  chat with a "Speaker 2" header and kept OFF the subtitles Scott sees. The
  service has no setting to say "there is only one speaker", so speaker
  separation is now switched off. Everything picked up by the microphone is
  treated as the person holding the phone.
- Side effect: someone else in the room speaking near the phone is now
  captioned as you, without the "Speaker 2" header.

Microphone missing from a video call — NOT fixed, needs one more data point
- I did not find the cause in the code. The translation tap reads the mic
  before the call sends it, so a check for next time it happens: look at the
  chat.
  - If the chat is filling with your transcript while the other person hears
    nothing: the mic works and the audio is lost on the way out (mute, route,
    or network). Tell me that, plus whether the Bose were connected.
  - If the chat is ALSO empty while translation is on: the microphone input
    itself is dead. Tell me which audio output was selected (speaker, phone,
    Bose) and whether switching it brought the mic back.
- Under the hood, the mic tap was also made safer: it now hands the audio off
  immediately instead of doing work on the call's audio thread, and a race
  between the audio thread and switching translation off was removed.
