Build 18 - call translation fix

Fixed: a finished sentence sometimes was not translated until you said another word. The transcription service decides by itself when a sentence is over, and after a pause it was sometimes left waiting. Now, if the live caption has not changed for 2 seconds, the app tells the service the sentence is finished, and the translation follows about half a second later. The next word takes about one second longer to appear, while the connection reopens.

Side effect: if you stop in the middle of a sentence for more than 2 seconds, it is split into two messages.

Also in this build, everything from build 17: the Video Call Heat settings, the automatic throttling when the phone is hot, and the temperature badge on the call screen.

Please check: after a long pause, the last sentence reaches the chat and the subtitles by itself, without saying anything else.
